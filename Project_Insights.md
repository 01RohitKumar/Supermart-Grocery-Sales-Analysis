# Project Insights

## 1. Sales Performance
- Total Sales: ₹14,956,982
- Total Profit: ₹3,747,121.20
- Total Orders: 9,994
- Average Discount: 22.68%

## 2. Category Insights
- **Eggs, Meat & Fish** recorded the highest sales among the categories.
- **Snacks** generated the highest profit among the categories.

## 3. Regional Insights
- The **West** region recorded the highest sales.
- North region sales are unusually low compared with other regions. The source data should be reviewed before making regional business decisions.

## 4. Time-Based Insights
- **2018** was the highest-sales year in the dataset.
- **November** recorded the highest monthly sales.

## 5. Business Recommendations
- Investigate the reasons behind low sales in the North region and validate the underlying data.
- Analyze the pricing, product mix, and discounts for high-sales categories.
- Study the factors contributing to Snacks' profitability and consider applying relevant strategies to other categories.
- Review monthly sales trends to plan inventory and promotions ahead of peak periods.

## Conclusion
This Excel project demonstrates data cleaning, KPI tracking, PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, and interactive dashboard development to support data-driven business decisions.
